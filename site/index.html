The mistakes this page exists to stop

One vendor, two different minutes. Deepgram prints $0.075 per minute at the top of its pricing page, and that number is its Voice Agent — speech-to-text plus a managed language model plus synthetic speech. Its speech-to-text alone is $0.0036–$0.0092 per minute. Quoting the agent rate as an STT rate makes Deepgram look about 17x more expensive than it is in the job most people actually give it. Both are on the page; only one is labelled as such in the table below.

Two vendors, two different units. ElevenLabs and Deepgram are quoted in characters vs minutes, so a like-for-like comparison has to pass through a characters-per-minute assumption, and that assumption swings 600–1,000 characters per minute depending on the speaker. At 900 chars/min, ElevenLabs v4 speech costs $0.0720 per spoken minute and Deepgram Aura-2 costs $0.0270 — a 2.7x gap. But an agent minute is not a speech minute: ElevenLabs Agents is $0.080/min and Deepgram's Voice Agent Standard is $0.075/min, a 6.7% gap in the other direction. If you price an agent from per-character rates you are comparing the wrong two numbers.
