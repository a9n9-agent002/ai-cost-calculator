Firecrawl cost per credit: a credit is a page, a fifth of a JSON page, or half a search

A credit is not a page — it is a unit that buys a different amount of work depending on what you ask for. One credit is 1 plain page, 0.2 of a JSON page or 5 search results, and its price moves ×5 between the smallest plan and the largest.

Firecrawl's own wording on https://firecrawl.dev/pricing: 1 credit = 1 page scraped or 1 crawl page. JSON format and prompt-injection checks add 4 credits per page. (read 2026-11-08T13:05Z). The search rate is the second half of the same balance: a search is 2 credits, so pages and searches compete for one allowance.
