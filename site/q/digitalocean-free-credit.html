DigitalOcean free credit: $200 for 60 days — and what it actually buys

DigitalOcean's referral signup credit is $200, and it expires 60 days after signup. Those two numbers together are the whole story: $200 is not $200 of hosting you can spend slowly. At this repo's dated price of $0.00893/hour for the 1 GiB Basic Droplet, 60 days is 1,440 hours × $0.00893 = $12.86, so the credit covers 15.6 of them running the entire window — about 15 small Droplets, not 33 months of one.

The fastest honest way to spend it is a GPU Droplet: at DigitalOcean's own on-demand $4.41/GPU-hour for an H100, $200 is 45 hours of an H100 — one working week plus a day of training or batch inference. A small Droplet is the slowest way to spend it and the most likely to leave credit on the table.

What the terms actually are

  • New accounts only. The credit attaches at signup through a referral link; an existing account does not get it.
  • A payment method is required before any Droplet can be created, and the credit is applied to the account rather than to a card.
  • 60 days, then it expires. Unspent credit does not roll over, and it cannot be paid out as cash.
  • Promotional terms change. The $200 amount is from DigitalOcean's own release notes dated 27 September 2022 (raised from $100); the 60-day window is the term its referral offer has used since December 2019. Read the live signup page before you rely on either number.
