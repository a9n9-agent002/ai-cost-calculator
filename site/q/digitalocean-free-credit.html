DigitalOcean free credit: $5, $100 or $200 — the window decides, not the amount

A new DigitalOcean account does not get one credit. On 2026-11-09 at least three offers are live at once: $5 for 90 days on the default signup flow, $100 for 60 days on DigitalOcean's own trial landing page, and $200 on partner and startup tracks. Because each month is capped at the plan's monthly price, the number that decides what a credit is worth is amount ÷ window — the monthly spend that would consume it before it expires. $5 over 90 days needs $1.69 a month; $100 over 60 days needs $50.69; $200 over 60 days needs $101.39. The cheapest Droplet on the list is $4.00 a month, so the default credit cannot cover a full window at any size — and the largest offers cannot be spent in full on a small one.

Where each number comes from

The $5 / 90 days figure is documented: DigitalOcean's Signup Credit page, stamped Last verified 15 Jul 2026, applies it automatically to a first team, requires a payment method, excludes SaaS Add-Ons and says it is not transferable. The $100 / 60 days figure is on DigitalOcean's own try.digitalocean.com/paas page, read on 2026-11-09: "$100, 60-day free trial requires valid credit card", with overage billed to the card. The $200 figure has exactly one first-party document behind it — the release note of 27 September 2022, which says referral signups "receive a $200 account credit, increased from $100" — plus partner pages and a DigitalOcean community profile that still advertise it. The Promotional Credit & Discount Terms (Last updated 7 June 2024) cover all of them: no cash value, non-transferable, one code per account, and Marketplace, Support, crypto and upfront prepay spending ineligible. Terms are "subject to change by posting", which is exactly how a 2022 $200 becomes a 2026 $5 without anybody misquoting anything.

So the honest answer to "how much free credit does DigitalOcean give?" is that it depends which page you sign up on, and the balance in the control panel's top bar is the only number that applies to you. On GPUs the credit is hours and minutes

A single 80 GB H100 Droplet is $4.41 per GPU-hour on demand and an L40S $1.57 (DigitalOcean GPU Droplets pricing, read 2026-11-08). GPU Droplets bill for a 5-minute minimum and keep billing while powered off, so a mis-mounted run can spend a large share of a small credit before anyone notices.

What to check before you sign up

  • Read the balance, not the blog post. The credit appears in the control panel as "Signup Credit" as soon as the team exists. That number is the offer you got, and it is the only one your build should be sized against.
  • A payment method comes first. The $5 and $100 offers both require a card, PayPal, Google Pay or Apple Pay before a Droplet can exist; the $5 Managed Agents trial is the one no-card path, and it is sized for testing, not hosting.
  • Decide against the window, not the amount. On the $6 plan, $5 covers 27.8% of 90 days, $100 covers the whole 60-day window with $88.00 left over, and $200 leaves $188.00. If your plan was "run it free for two months", the plan was built on a number no current page promises.
  • Unused credit expires. It has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and one code applies per account.
