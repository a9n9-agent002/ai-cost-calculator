The cheapest hour is not the cheapest bill

CoreWeave's HGX H100 (8-GPU node) is priced by the node: $49.24 for 8 GPUs is $6.16 per GPU-hour, but the smallest purchase is $49.24. One GPU-hour there costs what 19.7 hours of Hyperstack's $2.50 card cost. Lambda sells one GPU by the hour, and charges for the privilege: the 1× SXM instance is 6.5% more per GPU-hour than its own 8× instance ($4.89 vs $4.59). If your job is one GPU for two hours, the cheapest quote per hour can be the most expensive way to run it.

SXM, NVL, PCIe: the same model in three shapes

At RunPod the SXM card is 38.1% more than the PCIe card ($3.99 vs $2.89); at Hyperstack the SXM hour is $3.20 against $2.50 for PCIe, with an NVLink variant at $2.60 between them. These are not interchangeable products — the interconnect differs — and this page does not measure throughput, so it cannot tell you whether the SXM premium pays for itself. It can tell you that a "per GPU-hour" quote with no variant is half a price.

The cheapest hour on this page is a spot hour

Novita's interruptible SXM hour is $1.70 — 49.9% below its own on-demand price of $3.39, and below every on-demand listing here. CoreWeave's spot hour is $2.464 per GPU on the same 8-GPU node minimum ($19.71/hour). Both are interruptible: the provider may reclaim the machine, so a spot hour is a price for work you checkpoint, not for work with a deadline. The spot discount is also the one number vendors change without notice.

A reservation is a utilisation bet

Hyperstack publishes both an on-demand and a reservation price for the same card, which makes the break-even visible. It is the hourly saving multiplied by the hours you actually use:

Between 70% and 85% of every hour in the month has to be busy before the reservation beats paying on demand — 511 to 620 hours, about 17 to 20 hours a day. Below that, the reservation is the more expensive choice, and it is still the more expensive choice if the job finishes in a weekend.

Billing granularity turns seconds into money

Take 1,000 jobs of 40 seconds each: 11.11 GPU-hours of real work. Billed per second (RunPod, Novita, DigitalOcean) that is $27.78 at $2.50/hour. Billed per minute (Hyperstack) every job rounds up to a minute — 16.67 GPU-hours, 50% more, $41.67. The rounding rule adds $13.89 over these 1,000 jobs — about a third of what choosing between this page's cheapest and dearest hours is worth for the same work, which is exactly the decision a per-hour table invites you to make.

What this page does not know

No throughput benchmarks: SXM has more memory bandwidth than a PCIe card and this page does not measure how much faster a job runs on either, so it cannot price performance. No availability: a listed price you cannot get a machine at is not a price you pay, and none of these pages publishes stock. No storage or egress: the per-hour rate excludes volumes, object storage, images and data transfer, which for a 100-hour job can be a real line. Vast.ai's marketplace prices are set per host and are not in the HTML its pricing page serves, so it is absent from the table rather than measured at zero. Lambda's figures carry a footnote: they take effect 2026-11-16, so a reader before that date sees different numbers in their console. Nothing on this page was rented or benchmarked.
