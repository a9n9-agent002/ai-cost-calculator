Which LLM API is cheapest? The count, per provider

Asked as a search question — which LLM API is the cheapest provider — the answer is not one name. Counted on output price at headline rates, Novita 8, DeepInfra 14, OpenRouter 12 of 34. When every provider is priced at its cheapest routeable endpoint instead of its model-level headline, it moves to Novita 4, DeepInfra 9, OpenRouter 21 — because OpenRouter's model-level price is not its cheapest endpoint in most of these models, so a comparison built from headline prices flatters the other two.

At the specific mix in the table the picture is sharper: on 4 of 34 models Novita has the lowest monthly bill, DeepInfra 8 and OpenRouter 13, and the remaining rows are ties — the same model at two or three providers for the same money, which is what an open-weight model eventually becomes. A tie is marked on every tied cell rather than awarding it to the first column.

That is the honest reading of a table that earns from one of the three: Novita is the cheapest of these providers on a minority of models, and the rows where it loses are printed, not hidden. A comparison that only showed the vendor it earns from would be an advertisement.

What this does not measure

Price is not the product. This page has no data on output quality, latency, throughput, rate limits, uptime, context-window honesty or data handling, and no account exists here to test any of them, so cost is a tiebreaker after those, not a ranking. Prices are also list prices: no committed-use discount, no negotiated rate, and no volume tier is modelled. Cache-read prices differ per provider and are not in the monthly figure above — a workload that re-reads a long prompt will move on that line.
