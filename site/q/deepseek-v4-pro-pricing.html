The winner depends on the mix, not just the model

Divided by the output count, each provider is a straight line — input price × r + output price, where r is input tokens per output token. Interleaved, the lines give the answer at every mix rather than at one: at every input:output ratio, OpenRouter.

Novita is dearer on both axes — $1.6 vs $0.211 per million input tokens and $3.2 vs $0.422 per million output — so no input:output ratio makes it the cheaper bill.

Model-level prices are not always the cheapest price a provider will route to. OpenRouter's cheapest routeable endpoint prices this mix at $12.57, so the headline bill overstates what routing actually costs there.

What this does not measure

Price is not the product: no account exists on this site to measure quality, latency, throughput, rate limits or data handling, so cost is a tiebreaker after those, not a ranking.
