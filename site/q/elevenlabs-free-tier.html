ElevenLabs free tier: 10,000 characters a month, worth $0.80

Yes, there is a free plan, and it is small: 10,000 characters a month of v4 speech, which the vendor's own minute column prints as ~10 minutes. On v4 Turbo the same free plan lists 20,000 characters and ~20 minutes, because that model's rate is half. Both allowances are the same money: $0.80 a month at the vendor's own rates.

The free tier is a $0.80 credit, printed as whatever number of characters that buys on the model you pick. Both rows multiply out to the same dollar figure, which is why the characters column doubles when the rate halves — and why "the free plan gives you 10,000 characters" is only true on the dearer model. At the 900-characters-per-minute read this site uses elsewhere, the same 10,000 characters is about 11 finished minutes — the vendor's ~10 assumes exactly 1,000 characters a minute — and that is the whole free month.

What the free plan does not include

The paid plans' own feature lists answer this without interpretation: Commercial License and Instant Voice Cloning are listed as "Everything in free, plus" on Starter, so neither is part of free. Starter is $6 a month billed monthly — the plan card prints first month $1 — and it includes 75,000 characters, 7.5× the free allowance for a dollar. Creator is $22 (first month $11) for 275,000.

Where the free tier stops being enough: a 10-minute-a-month allowance is one short video, or a single demo call. Anything that runs monthly — a narration series, an agent in staging, an audiobook — crosses into the paid ladder immediately, and the ladder is block-priced, so the plan you pick should be chosen from finished minutes, not from the plan nearest your budget. If the product is a startup rather than a test, the 12-month grant is a different decision and a much larger number.
