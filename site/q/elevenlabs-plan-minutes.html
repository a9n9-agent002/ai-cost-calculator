That is the trap the plan table hides. On the vendor's own 1,000-characters-a-minute column, 300 finished minutes and 1,238 finished minutes both land on the $99.00 block, so the same monthly price buys four times the audio. On the 900-character basis this page's per-minute prices use, the same pair is 306 and 1,376 minutes — a plan buys 11.1% more minutes than the vendor's column claims. On Creator the boundary is 305.6 finished minutes: 305 cost $22.00 and 306 cost $99.00 — 4.5x the bill for one more minute. And needing only 25 minutes past a block charges the whole step up: on Creator those 25 minutes cost $77.00, which is $3.08 per minute against a plan average of $0.08 (and $27.64 per minute at the top of the ladder).

Work out your finished minutes at 900 characters per spoken minute (the vendor's column divides by 1,000, which overstates the characters a given duration needs by 10.0%), then check which block you land in — not which plan looks closest.
