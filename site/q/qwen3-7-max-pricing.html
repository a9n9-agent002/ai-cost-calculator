Divided by the output count, each provider is a straight line — input price × r + output price, where r is input tokens per output token. Interleaved, the lines give the answer at every mix rather than at one: at every input:output ratio, Novita.

What this does not measure

Price is not the product: no account exists on this site to measure quality, latency, throughput, rate limits or data handling, so cost is a tiebreaker after those, not a ranking.
