Break-even is where the commitment's monthly total equals the on-demand total for the hours you would actually use: reserved × 730 ÷ on-demand. Across these cards it lands at roughly 74–76% utilisation — about 18 hours a day, every day, for a year. Below that, reserved costs more than paying on demand, and it costs the whole commitment for a job that lasts a weekend.

Same card, two clouds

L40S 48 GB: $0.55 at Novita vs $1.57 at DigitalOcean — a 2.85× spread for the same GPU model. Hourly price is also not job cost: storage, egress, idle time between jobs and the interruption rate on spot are all real lines that a per-hour table cannot show.

What this page does not know

Novita's GPU page does not publish per-GPU vCPU/RAM/disk specs, so its rows carry prices only; DigitalOcean's page does not state whether a powered-off reserved GPU keeps billing, which is why the commitment total above is the commitment's own arithmetic and not an invoice. Spot rates are interruptible: the provider may reclaim the instance, so a spot price is not a price for a deadline. Nothing here was rented.
