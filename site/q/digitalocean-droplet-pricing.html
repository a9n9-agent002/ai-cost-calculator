DigitalOcean droplet pricing: 31 plans, and the $6.00 cap that binds at 672 hours

A "$6 droplet" is not $6 a month. The 1 GiB Basic plan is $0.00893/hour against a $6.00 monthly figure, and 0.00893 × 730 = $6.52. The two numbers only agree after 671.9 hours of runtime — so the monthly price is a ceiling that binds late in the month, and anything shorter is billed by the hour at 8.6% more than the cap implies.

Backups are a percentage of the Droplet, not a flat add-on

DigitalOcean prices backups at 30% daily or 20% weekly of the Droplet's own price, so the "$6 droplet" becomes $7.80 with daily backups and $5.20 for the $4.00 0.5 GiB plan. On ten Droplets that checkbox is the difference between $60.00 and $78.00 a month.

The same table by RAM alone says something the plan order does not: the rate is not monotonic in size. The 0.5 GiB plan costs $8.00 per GiB against $6.00 per GiB on the 1 GiB plan, and the cheapest RAM rate on the page is $5.25 per GiB (16 GiB, $84.00, Memory-Optimized) — 1.52× the rate of the smallest plan. A 0.5 GiB Droplet is still the right purchase for a small service; it is just the most expensive way to buy memory here.

Short-lived jobs are priced differently again: 0.00595 × 40 hours = $0.24 for a two-day test, against $4.00 if you forget to destroy it. The monthly figure never appears on a 40-hour job's bill, and the hourly figure never appears on a long-running one's.
