Firecrawl search cost: 2 credits per 10 results, and 12 credits per answered question

A search is 2 credits per 10 results — and searches share the same credit balance as pages, so a plan's "5,000 credits" is 5,000 pages or 2,500 searches, not both. An agent that searches and then scrapes its ten hits spends 12 credits per question: 2 for the search, 10 for the pages.
