The hours claim is on a slower reading rate than the plan table

The grant page says 33,000,000 characters is "over 680 hours of Agents usage". At the 1,000-characters-per-spoken-minute basis the plan table uses, the same characters are 550 hours (33,000,000 ÷ 1,000 ÷ 60). To reach 680 hours the read has to be 809 characters a minute.
